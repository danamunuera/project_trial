Part 2:
- We based our work on the Barcelona Airbnb listings dataset. A second dataset is yet to be added.
- Our main variables of interest were price per person, accommodation capacity and neighbourhood.
- We narrowed our scope to entire homes and apartments accommodating 2 to 6 people in Eixample, Ciutat Vella, Gràcia and Sarrià-Sant Gervasi.
- Our initial graph reveals that median price per person generally decreases as accommodation capacity increases, except in Ciutat Vella after three guests.
